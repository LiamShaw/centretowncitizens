The CCA Seniors Committee working with the City of Ottawa and TD Canada Trust to transform the corner of Elgin and Somerset into a Seniors Corner. We’re working to remove two dead tree stumps, relocate planter boxes, cover the plastic PVC tubing and wires, and repairing uneven paving stones. Once this project is completed, there will a nice corner for Centretown seniors to sit and relax!
