#### Our Seniors Committee is committed to representing seniors in our Centretown community through outreach, advocacy, and social events.

Our mandate includes:

- Making seniors feel welcome in the community by holding regular social functions;
- Facilitating community involvement and encouraging seniors’ participation in activities;
- Organizing awareness about seniors’ issues through advocacy and information sessions;
- Working with other organizations in order to make Centretown a desirable and safe community for seniors.
Interested in joining this committee? Become a [CCA member](/membership/) today and contact us at [ccca@centretowncitizens.ca](mailto:ccca@centretowncitizens.ca?subject=Seniors%20Committee).
