On Victoria Day, member of the Seniors and Trees and Greenspace Committees  gathered at the N.E. corner of Elgin and Somerset Streets to clean up the corner and plant flowers. What a difference it has made! The wires that have been sticking out of the ground for 2.5 years are the next things to get cleaned up. The group is looking for 2 park benches to install between the planter boxes. Thank you to everyone who has been supporting this Elgin Street beautification project by the CCA.
