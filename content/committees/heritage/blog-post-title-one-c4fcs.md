In July 2020, the Heritage Working Group send a letter to the City of Ottawa’s Heritage Planning Department with a list of several recommendations. The CCA recommended that:

- That the city consider a more nuanced classification scheme for its historic properties;
- That the boundaries of the proposed Dundonald Park Heritage Conservation District (HCD) be enlarged;
- That the Golden Triangle neighbourhood be considered for HCD status;
- That city staff continues to engage with the public before recommendations go to city council.
You can read the full letter [here](/files/CCA-Letter-to-City-of-Ottawa-Heritage-Planning.pdf).
