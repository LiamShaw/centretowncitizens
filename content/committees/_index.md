The Centretown Community Association has a number of very active committees that deal with a wide range of issues. Our committees are an integral part of our organization, and they provide an opportunity to delve deep into specific topics, issues, and initiatives. Everyone is welcome to attend meetings and share ideas on how to make our community a better place to live.

To find out more about our various committees and how you can participate, please visit the committee pages below!
