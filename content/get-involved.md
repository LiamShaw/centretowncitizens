### Membership

Membership is open to everyone who lives or works in Centretown. Our paid membership allows you to run for our Board of Directors, vote at our Annual General Meeting, and helps to promote the interests of our community as it advocates for Centretown.

[Become a Member](/membership/) or [Renew your Membership](/membership/)

![](/images/Volunteer-1606338376084.jpg)

### Volunteer

Are you looking for a way to help out in your community? The CCA is always looking for volunteers to help engage with community members, assist in running events, and help contribute in making Centretown a better place to live, work, and play.

[Sign-up to Volunteer](/volunteer/)

![](/images/unsplash-image-K0E6E0a0R3A-1623957955163.jpg)

### Donate

Your donation will help us to run neighbourhood events like Earth Days and Fall Festivals, community conversations and workshops on local issues, and many other initiatives that help make Centretown a better place to live, work, and play. Any amount helps!

[Donate to the CCA](/donate/)

![](/images/Sponsorship-1607366255685.png)

### Sponsorship

Local business plays a vital role in making Centretown a great place to live. Through your sponsorship of the CCA, you can help to improve our community while promoting your business to residents and visitors to Centretown.

[Become a Sponsor](/sponsorship/)
