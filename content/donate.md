### Donate to the CCA

Your donation will help the CCA to run neighbourhood events like Earth Day, community workshops, and other initiatives that help make Centretown a better place to live, work, and play. Any amount helps!

*Please note that donations to the CCA are not tax deductible.

You’ll be redirected to Paypal upon form submission to complete your donation.
