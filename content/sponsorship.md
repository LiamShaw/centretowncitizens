# Become a Sponsor

### Apply to be a Sponsor

Local business plays an essential role in making Centretown a vibrant and dynamic community.  By sponsoring the CCA, you can help to engage and improve our community, and promote your business to residents and visitors to Centretown. Let’s work together to make our neighbourhood better!
