### Apply to Volunteer

Are looking for way to help out in your community? The CCA is always looking for volunteers for help in running events and engaging with the community.

Over the past few years, our volunteers have:

-Assisted with the Centretown Community Support Network to assist those affected by the COVID-19 pandemic;

-Helped to run events like our Community Conversations series, which brings in prominent community and civic leaders to discuss issues facing the Centretown community;

-Worked with the CCA on community outreach during events like Capital Pride, Earth Day, and the Minto Park Plant Sale.

If you’d like to help out, please fill out the volunteer form!
