This is a walking tour in aid of establishing the proposed western boundary (in the Gladstone-Queensway area) for a new Heritage Conservation District in West Centretown. Meet in Dundonald Park (Somerset and Lyon) at 2 p.m. The walking tour will proceed block by block. Information will be provided on the heritage characteristics and ages of the Victorian houses on each block. Participants will be given a ballot and asked to vote on whether each block should be included in the new HCD.
