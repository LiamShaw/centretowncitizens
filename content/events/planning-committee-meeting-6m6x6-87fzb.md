  About the event

Michelle Hurtubise, the Executive Director at Centretown Community Health Centre*, will discuss ”how effectively policing is meeting community needs and producing outcomes that actually keep Ottawa safer.”

About the speaker

Michelle Hurtubise brings more than 30 years of experience working with community-based health and social services. She brings a passion for health equity, community development and social justice to all.

Michelle is committed to volunteering and has sat on numerous boards of directors in accreditation, child welfare, violence against women and the non-profit sector generally. She is currently an active volunteer with the Loran Scholars Foundation and Up With Women.

*To learn more about the Centretown Community Health Centre, please visit [www.centretownchc.org](http://www.centretownchc.org/)
