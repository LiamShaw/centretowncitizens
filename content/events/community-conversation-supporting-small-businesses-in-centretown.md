Please join us on Tuesday. March 30 at 7:00pm for our upcoming Community Conversation about supporting small businesses in Centretown! We'll be joined by representatives from local BIA's and small business owners.

We'll discuss how businesses have adapted to COVID-19, what new initiatives are being planned, and how the community can help to ensure that small businesses remain an integral part of our Centretown community.
