#### Ottawa, or Bytown as it first was known, was born in 1826 — 200 years ago.

On the walking tour, history raconteur Jack Hanna tells of the Algonquins who lived here around 1826, and the fate of the great chief Constant Pinesi and his people.

The creation of Bytown was dramatic; it was an instant boomtown. Of course there were heroes and rogues, scandal and daring, fools and fortunes. Learn how Col. John By’s design of Bytown has fundamentally shaped Ottawa, right up to today.

The CCA’s Ottawa 200 walking tour is offered on Saturday, August 15.

Meet at the Colonel John By statue in Major’s Hill Park (behind the Chateau Laurier). The tour starts at 10:30 a.m. It lasts about 90 minutes and covers 1.4 kms.

There is no fee, but donations to the Centretown Community Association are appreciated.
