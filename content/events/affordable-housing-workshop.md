Join us for a community conversation on Affordable Housing.

In response to the numerous questions and comments raised at our Annual General Meeting earlier this year, the release of the Official Plan, and the impact of projected densification on Ottawa's neighbourhoods in the coming decades, we are hosting a workshop on the subject of Affordable Housing.
Joined by special guests MPP Joel Harden and Somerset Ward Councillor Catherine McKenney, we will discuss existing and emerging types of affordable housing, and provide an overview of policy and planning supports around this highly important type of social infrastructure.
Topics will include:
-Types of Affordable Housing
-Social Housing
-Responsibility and Funding
-The Housing Crisis
-Strategies and Solutions
-Community Conversation
