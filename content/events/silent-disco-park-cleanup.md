Do you consider yourself a creative person or a lover of dance? Do you care for the environment through acts of service? Are you curious about new ways to practice mindfulness and play?!

Join us for a FREE silent disco park clean up, where you’ll be able to listen to music with other participants using Bluetooth headphones while clearing McNabb park of litter and trash. Featured channel will be a live DJ set courtesy of DJ Del Pilar.

Not interested in dancing? Drop by to chat with community volunteers who are passionate about ending plastic pollution and promoting circular economies.

6:15 pm – 8 pm Tuesday May 13th 2025 – McNabb Park (180 Percy St)

Rain date: Wednesday May 14th 2025, 6:15 pm - 8 pm

Headphones, trash bags, gloves and a limited number of trash pickers will be provided - Bring water, comfortable shoes, and a piece of ID for your headphones deposit!

Although registration is not required, you can secure your headset by registering [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSedgieyXZs-7Ps9FyIDidqb7Xu7p2tJgdfK2QGWf8LJNBrE9Q/viewform?usp=sharing)!
