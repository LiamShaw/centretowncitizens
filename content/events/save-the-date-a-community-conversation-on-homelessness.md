Come join us for a Community Conversation on Homelessness in Ottawa Centre organized by the Centretown Community Association, the Centretown Community Health Centre, the Somerset West Community Health Centre, and the Dalhousie Community Association.

We will discuss with:

- MP Yasir Naqvi
- MPP Joel Harden
- Councillor Ariel Troster
and leading homelessness and housing experts from Operation Come Home, the Ottawa Mission, Salus and the CCOC to address this important issue in our community.

This event on January 15, from 6 to 8:30pm will be both in-person and virtual., to provide a platform for open dialogue, sharing ideas, and creating solutions. Let's come together to make a positive impact and support those in need.

Don't miss out on this opportunity to be part of the conversation and change!
