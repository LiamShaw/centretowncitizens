This year the Minto Park Plant Sale put on by the Trees, Greenspace and Sustainability/Climate Change Committee of the CCA will take place on SATURDAY, JUNE 7, 2025 FROM 9AM TO 3PM.  PLEASE MARK YOUR CALENDARS!

There will be a variety of annuals, perennials, herbs, vegetable plants and house plants.

If you have any plants to donate, please e-mail ccca@centretowncitizens.ca .
