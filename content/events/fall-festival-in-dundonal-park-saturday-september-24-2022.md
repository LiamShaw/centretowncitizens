CCA's Trees and Greenspace Committee will be hosting the annual Fall Festival at Dundonald Park (Somerset and Lyon St.) on Saturday, September 24, 2022, 10 am - 2 pm.

Rain date: Sunday, September 25, 2022

Family friendly activities for all ages will be offered: a drawing workshop in honour of Annie Pootoogook by the Ottawa Art Gallery; music and dance by Chinese Seniors; and bilingual storytime courtesy of the Ottawa Public Library. Get aquainted with Bibliobike, the Tool Lending Library, Grassroots Group, Ticket Defence Program, and others.
