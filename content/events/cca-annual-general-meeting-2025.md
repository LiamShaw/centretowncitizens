The next Annual General Meeting of the Centretown Community Association will be held virtually on Wednesday, November 12, 2025 at 7:00pm.  The room will be open at 6:30pm for conversation and community networking.

To register for the event, please click on this link:  [Register For CCA Annual General Meeting on Wednesday, November 12, 2025](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/maQc_F0vRbepLJdj1IloQA?mc_cid=7a991b9bec&mc_eid=UNIQID) .   You will receive the link to the Zoom meeting via e-mail after registering.

#### KEYNOTE SPEAKER:

#### JACK HANNA, Chair of the CCA Heritage Committee

#### Topic: Ottawa’s 200th Birthday

Our keynote address will be given by Jack Hanna on the 200th Birthday of Ottawa in 2026.  The City of Ottawa staff will also give a brief presentation on Heritage Districts and the two new Heritage Conservation Districts that will be located in Centretown

All current members of the CCA are eligible to vote.  To purchase or renew your membership, please go to   [Membership — Centretown Community Association (centretowncitizens.ca)](/membership/)

 Membership in the CCA is open to anyone who lives or works or owns property in Centretown. Our neighbourhood is bounded by Bronson Avenue on the west, the Rideau Canal on the east, the Queensway on the south, and the Ottawa River on the north.

 Annual memberships expire on December 31st.

The AGM will elect the President and new Directors of the Board.
