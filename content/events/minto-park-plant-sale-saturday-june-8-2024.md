This year the Minto Park Plant Sale put on by the Trees, Greenspace and Sustainability Committee of the CCA will take place on SATURDAY, JUNE 8, 2024 FROM 9AM TO 3PM.  PLEASE MARK YOUR CALENDARS!

There will be a variety of annuals, perennials, herbs, vegetable plants and house plants.

If you have any plants to donate, please contact Michelle Kirkman or Stephen Thirlwall via ccca@centretowncitizens.ca
