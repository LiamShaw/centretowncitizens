Join us for the 2022 Centretown Community Association AGM! Everyone is welcome.

The AGM will be Jan 26, 2022 at 7:00 PM Eastern Time.

Agenda:

- Get to know the new MP, Yasir Naqvi, and his positions on issues. Mr. Naqvi will be interviewed by CCA Vice-president Jack Hanna.
- Hear about the multitude of CCA activities over the past year, and what is planned for the year ahead
- Elect the CCA’s President and Board of Directors. Here is the detailed agenda for the meeting.
