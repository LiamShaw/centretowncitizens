The Planning Committee of the Centretown Community Association (CCA) meets via [Zoom](https://us06web.zoom.us/j/83320439697?pwd=MrKI9HkyuBk8bdhrBysflbVQ8el9hv.1)Tuesday, Feb. 6, at 7:00 p.m. On the agenda:

- A presentation by staff of the Ottawa Public Library , on OPL’s plans for Centretown after the closing of the main branch on Metcalfe;
- A presentation by the developer of 145 Waverley (just east of Cartier; photo below). The developer wishes to demolish a small brick house dating from the period 1875 to 1914, and replace it with an eight-unit apartment building.
