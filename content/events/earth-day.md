The Centretown Community Health Centre and the Centretown Community Association’s Trees and Greenspace Committee will be the annual Earth Day Festival at Dundonald Park (Somerset and Lyon Street) on Saturday, April 22, 2023 from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm.

In case of rain, the event will take place on April 23, 2023, at the same time and same location.
