  Ottawa’s municipal election is just around the corner, and residents will have an opportunity to hear directly from four mayoral candidates at a community debate on Thursday, October 15, 2026, at 7 pm at Knox Presbyterian Church, at Elgin and Lisgar Streets.

[Mark Sutcliffe](https://marksutcliffe.ca), [Jeff Leiper](https://www.leiper2026.ca), [Alex Lawson](https://votealex.ca) and [Neil Saravanamuttoo](https://neilformayor.ca) have confirmed that they will participate.

The debate is being hosted by the Centretown Community Association and other community associations in central Ottawa and will focus on issues important to residents across our communities.

CBC’s Hallie Cotnam will moderate the debate, which will also be livestreamed by Rogers TV.

Have a question for the candidates? Submit it in advance to [electiondebate@glebeca.ca](mailto:electiondebate@glebeca.ca).
