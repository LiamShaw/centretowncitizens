The Elgin Street Market is back for its fifth season in Boushey Square at the corner of Elgin and Waverly.

The market is the only farmer's market in Centretown, and will run every Sunday from June 15, 2025 to October 12, 2025 from 9am to 1pm, rain or shine.

As always, the market will feature a range of vendors: fresh produce, prepared foods, baked goods, coffee, various crafts, and other items.

We will also be conducting yoga by donation in Jack Purcell Park at 10 am on Sunday mornings, beginning June 29th through to September.

Back by popular demand will also be Jack Hanna's history walking tours, leaving from the market each week at 11am with rotating itineraries from week to week.

We are looking forward to a fantastic market season and hope to see you there!
