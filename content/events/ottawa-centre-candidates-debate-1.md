### Tuesday, April 15th, 7:00pm

### Doors open at 6:30pm

Knox Presbyterian Church

120 Lisgar Street (Elgin & Lisgar)

Parking is available at the City Hall parkade for 2$ or on the street for free after 5:30pm

This venue is wheelchair-accessible.

#### Invited Candidates Include:

#### Yasir Naqvi (Liberal), Joel Harden (NDP), Paul D'‘Orsonnens (Conservative), and Amanda Rosenstock (Green)

Le débat se déroulera principalement en anglais avec quelques questions en français.

Les personnnes candidates sont encouragées à s’exprimer dans les deux langues officielles.

#### Can’t attend in person?

The debate will be recorded by RogersTV and video made available online following the event.

The debate will be moderated by David Reevely, author and reporter with The Logic.

#### To submit a question in advance, please email electiondebate@glebeca.ca
