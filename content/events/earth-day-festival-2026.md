Come out on Earth Day to help us to celebrate spring!

The annual Centretown Earth Day Festival is taking place in Dundonald Park, 516 Somerset Street West (Somerset and Lyon), on Saturday, April 18, 2026 (10 a.m. to 2  p.m.).

For the kids, there will be games, arts and crafts. For adults, dozens of organizations will host booths offering information and opportunities for discussion. We will also be collecting electronics for a community electronics recycling event.   Free refreshments and snacks will be provided.

The festival is sponsored by the Centretown Community Association and the Centretown Community Health Centre.

Rain date is Sunday, April 19, 2026
