September 8, 2026 — The CCA’s Transportation Committee chair Derrick Simpson [spoke to CBC Ottawa Morning](https://www.cbc.ca/listen/live-radio/1-100-ottawa-morning/clip/16235949-centretown-community-concerns-around-return-office) about concerns about traffic now that people are returning to the office.

March 19, 2026 —Calling for a more ambitious approach, the CCA [supports expanding](https://www.ctvnews.ca/ottawa/article/community-group-calls-for-expanded-bus-only-lanes-on-bank-street-in-the-glebe/) bus-only lanes on Bank Street to improve transit reliability, reduce delays and strengthen the busiest bus corridor in the city.

February 12, 2026 — The CCA’s Heritage Committee chair Jack Hanna [spoke to the Ottawa Citizen](https://ottawacitizen.com/news/committee-rejects-impossible-standard-heritage), endorsing the rejection of an “impossible standard” for heritage conservation districts and emphasizing the need for practical criteria that protect neighbourhood character.

June 16, 2025 —  As the city’s core becomes more dense, CCA Planning Committee co-chair Jack Hanna [spoke](https://www.cbc.ca/listen/cbc-podcasts/1420-this-is-ottawa) with Robyn Bresnahan, host of the This is Ottawa podcast, about what developers should consider when building in Centretown.

June 8, 2025 — Construction is underway at the derelict Somerset House on Bank Street, and CCA Heritage Working Group chair Jack Hanna [told](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/hope-for-somerset-house-renewed-as-major-construction-finally-underway-1.4956851) CBC that he’s cautiously optimistic about redevelopment plans for the building.

May 29, 2025 — The CCA is sponsoring Strong Towns Ottawa’s takeover of the Mayfair Theatre marquee, to raise awareness about the need for bus lanes on Bank Street. CCA Transportation Committee chair Derrick Simpson [discussed](https://www.ctvnews.ca/ottawa/article/community-group-takes-over-mayfair-theatre-marquee-to-promote-bank-street-bus-lane-pitch/?mc_cid=edd497ceed&mc_eid=61d6580eed) the issue with CTV.

May 22, 2025 — CCA Transportation Committee chair Derrick Simpson says “we can’t wait anymore” for the creation of dedicated bus corridors on Bank Street, in an [article](https://www.ledroit.com/actualites/actualites-locales/ottawa/2025/05/22/des-corridors-dedies-aux-autobus-reclames-sur-la-rue-bank-7FUP3WBT3JDJNPMI3NMOXN6VV4/) from Le Droit.
