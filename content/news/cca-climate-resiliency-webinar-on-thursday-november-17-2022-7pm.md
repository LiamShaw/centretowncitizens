  The Centretown Community Association in Collaboration
 with The Glebe Community Association invites you to attend our

#### Climate Resiliency Webinar

  Thursday, November 17, 2022
 7 - 9 pm

 Join us on Thursday, November 17, 2022, from 7 PM to 9 PM for a webinar on what Climate Resiliency can mean for Centretown. Our presenters include Professor Andy Kenney (Faculty of Forestry, UofT), architect Toon Dreessen, Birgit Isernhagen, Environmental Policy Lead at Ottawa Public Health, and landscape designer Mary Stewart. These experts will introduce a range of practical solutions to make our homes and our community more resilient to face the critical challenges of increased heat and storm events that climate change has already brought to our neighbourhood.
