It is with heavy the Centretown Community Association share the news of the passing of a remarkable individual. On August 27th, our community lost a leader and activist, former CCA Director Patricia Marsden Dole. Patricia's legacy in public service is unparalleled, having been Canada's first female Trade Commissioner in 1969 and serving with distinction in diplomacy from 1989 to 2003.

During her tenure on the CCA Board from 2007 to 2016, Patricia's dedication to advocating for seniors in Centretown was truly exceptional. She championed for improved accessibility, including wider sidewalks and safer intersections, advocating for the well-being of seniors with mobility challenges and urging the City Council to enhance support for the elderly in Ottawa.

In CCA Board meetings, Patricia's words resonated deeply, embodying wisdom and compassion. Patricia's heart was always leading her activism in Centretown and her kindness to others had an immediate impact when you met and held a discussion with her.

Though her presence in recent years was less frequent, the impact of her advocacy and professionalism reverberates throughout Centretown, earning her widespread respect and admiration within our community.

The CCA extends heartfelt condolences to Patricia's family during this difficult time. Her strength and support were unwavering pillars for her loved ones, and her absence will be deeply felt. To learn more about her remarkable life, you can read her obituary here:
