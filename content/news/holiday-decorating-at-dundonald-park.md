It’s the festive season & time to decorate the trees in the park with simple, homemade holiday ornaments and treats for the birds. The decorating party will take place Sunday, December 12, from 2 - 4 p.m. in Dundonald Park (at Somerset and Lyon).

Goodies for the birds might include popcorn strings, dried oranges, suet balls and such. Decorations that are neither fragile nor plastic, such as wooden or straw ornaments, are great too.

AND if you’re feeling the urge to bake, some people-type treats (cookies etc.) would not go amiss!

Let’s make Dundonald a winter wonderland — and a feast for birds!
