### Membership Sign-up and Renewal

Membership is our strength. By becoming a member of the Centretown Community Association, you can support the work of the CCA in running events and advocating for our community.

Membership in the CCA is open to anyone who lives or works in Centretown. Our neighbourhood is bounded by Bronson Avenue on the west, the Rideau Canal on the east, the Queensway on the south, and the Ottawa River on the north. You can find a map [here](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/1/edit?mid=1aNY5InbFDkdFiZcdvvPLVO8ZmCtoFDz2&usp=sharing).

All members have voting privileges at the Annual General Meeting and are eligible to serve on the CCA Board of Directors.
Annual memberships expire on December 31st.

A payment of a membership fee is required to confirm membership with the CCA.  Our suggested amount is $5.00.

Donations are appreciated.

You’ll be redirected to PayPal after the form is submitted to process your payment. You don’t need a PayPal account to use PayPal to process a credit card payment.

Your membership application is not complete until we have received your payment.   Once your payment has been processed you will receive a confirmation notice from PayPal. If you don’t receive the confirmation from PayPal your payment has not gone through.

If you do not wish to use PayPal please email us at ccca@centretowncitizens.ca for other methods of payment.

Thank you.
